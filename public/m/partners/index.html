Partner FAQ

Partner FAQ

Can we co-brand the referral experience?+

Yes. Strategic partners get a co-branded landing page and referral link. Your logo, your colors, your voice — pointing at our intake.

Do you share member contact info?+

Only with explicit member consent, and only the fields you need for follow-up. Members can revoke at any time, and you will see consent state on every record.

What notifications do we get?+

Email + in-portal alerts on every milestone: application started, enrolled, certification earned, placed. You choose digest cadence in your partner settings.

Can we bulk-import referrals?+

Yes — CSV upload with column mapping. We validate rows, deduplicate against existing members, and surface errors row-by-row before commit.

Is there a fee to be a partner?+

No. We do not charge partners to refer. Member services are funded by grants and partnerships, so there is no cost to members.

How do reports work?+

Pull placement, completion, and demographic reports straight from your partner dashboard. Schedule monthly exports to your inbox or your funder's data warehouse.
