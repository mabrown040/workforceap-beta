What happens next?

  1. Submit your application and tell us what kind of work or training you want.
  2. A WorkforceAP advisor reviews your information and follows up within 1–2 business days in most cases.
  3. We talk through fit, eligibility, and the best next step for your situation.
  4. If needed, we guide you to screening, documentation, interview, or program-readiness steps.
  5. You get a clearer recommendation for training, support, and job-readiness next steps.

Documentation we may review together

You don't need every document to start — nothing uploads on this page. If we move forward together, we sometimes ask for:

  • Government-issued photo ID
  • Proof of income or unemployment status (pay stub, unemployment letter, or self-certification)
  • Work authorization (if not a US citizen)
  • Social Security card or number (for tax reporting)

Don't worry if you don't have everything right now — our team will help.
