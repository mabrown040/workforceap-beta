01 — Brand Identity
Who We Are
WorkforceAP is a workforce development platform delivering free career training and industry certifications to underserved communities, veterans, and adult learners — starting in Austin, scaling nationwide.
Mission
Economic mobility through credible career pathways
We remove tuition as a barrier so that access to opportunity isn't limited by income. Training should be an investment, never a debt.
Audiences
Members · Employers · Partners
Job seekers who need structured pathways. Employers who need certified talent. Nonprofits and workforce centers who need scalable partnerships.
Creative North Star
The Digital Ivory Tower
Academic prestige meets modern workforce training. High-end editorial. Curated, authoritative, intentional. Not "startup cute," not "government dull."
Tagline
Empowering People. Advancing Futures.
Direct, civic, empowering. Serious without being bureaucratic. Built for people who have been overlooked — we actually get people hired.
Empowering People.
Advancing Futures.
Use this tagline consistently across all marketing surfaces. Never alter the word order. Pair with crimson on dark or white backgrounds.

06 — Design Principles
Rules & Principles
These rules define the Digital Ivory Tower aesthetic. They are non-negotiable guardrails, not preferences.
✓ Do
Use crimson (#ad2c4d) for all primary CTAs
Separate sections with tonal background shifts, not lines
Use glassmorphism for floating nav (bg/80, blur-12px)
Apply asymmetric layouts — off-center focal points create depth
Use rounded-xl (12px) for cards — rounded-md (6px) for buttons
Keep Inter as the only typeface
Use Material Symbols Outlined for icons throughout
Pair crimson + gold — never crimson + blue
✗ Don't
Use 1px solid borders to separate sections or cards
Use default Tailwind blue (#2563eb) as a primary CTA
Use pure black (#000000) for text — use #1c1b1b
Over-round corners — never apply the same large radius everywhere
Use decorative blobs, wavy dividers, or floating shapes
Center all content — asymmetry earns authority
Use emoji as design elements in UI
Mix warm and cool neutrals in the same surface
Elevation — Tonal Layering

Depth is achieved by stacking the surface-container tiers. Traditional shadows are a crutch for poor layout.

Accessibility

WCAG 2.1 AA compliance required. White on crimson (#ad2c4d) = 4.6:1. Crimson on white = 4.6:1. Minimum touch target: 48×48px. Focus ring: 0 0 0 2px white, 0 0 0 4px #ad2c4d. Never rely on color alone — pair with icon, label, or pattern.
