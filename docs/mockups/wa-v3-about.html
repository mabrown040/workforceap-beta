Our mission

Bridging the gap between education and industry.

WorkforceAP has spent 25+ years bridging the gap between education and industry, connecting skilled individuals with meaningful employment opportunities.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit built in Austin, our work is mission-driven: employer-aligned training and career support, with training and certificates offered at no cost to qualifying members through funded pathways.

We pair occupational training with practical career support — advisor guidance, resume and interview help, and job-search support — so that learning leads somewhere real.

Built in Austin, Texas
