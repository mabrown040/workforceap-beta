### Program Narrative (500 words)

**The Problem**

The Austin area faces a persistent skills gap. Employers struggle to find qualified technical talent. Job seekers face barriers to rapid, affordable training that employers actually value. Government workforce programs excel at volume but struggle with outcome measurement. Private bootcamps optimize for profit, not for community outcomes.

The gap isn't a knowledge problem—it's an **access problem**. Qualified training exists. So do jobs. What's missing is the bridge: *verified training that leads to documented employment*.

**Our Solution: WorkforceAP**

WorkforceAP is a platform built on a simple principle: **measure outcomes because your mission depends on them**.

We combine evidence-based curriculum (built on 12 years of Austin employment data) with modern technology (real-time tracking, employer integration, continuous iteration). The result is workforce development that's rapid, measurable, and employer-aligned.

**Proven Track Record**

Our founding evidence comes from Career Services Network, a 12-year Austin-based operation that trained 834 students:

- **94% employment placement** in technical and project management roles
- **152-day median completion** (faster than traditional degree pathways)
- **$37.50/hr median post-completion compensation** (range: $18–$72.50)
- **53 verified employers** across finance, tech, healthcare, manufacturing, energy
- **99% alumni contactability** for ongoing engagement

This wasn't luck. This was intentional program design, instructor quality, and employer partnership. Our job now is to make this repeatable, measurable, and scalable.

**Platform Innovation**

What CSN achieved with spreadsheets, WorkforceAP achieves with technology:

1. **Student Dashboard** — Real-time progress tracking, skill certification, employment status
2. **Employer Portal** — Post roles, source candidates, track placements, provide feedback on graduate skill gaps
3. **Outcome Measurement** — Automated 6-month and 12-month surveys, compensation tracking, career progression
4. **Curriculum Loop** — Monthly employer feedback synthesized into curriculum updates
5. **Alumni Network** — Long-term support, continuing education, career coaching

**Why This Matters**

Government funding typically measures workforce outcomes in quarterly reports. By then, outcomes are stale. Decisions lag by months.

WorkforceAP measures in real-time. An employer says "your graduates need better data analysis skills"? Curriculum adjusts within a month. A graduate hits a career wall at month 6? Coaching kicks in automatically.

The result: faster iteration, better outcomes, sustainable impact.

**Implementation Plan**

**Months 1–3:** Platform MVP (student tracking, 6-month survey framework, employer dashboard)

**Months 4–8:** First cohort enrollment and execution (50–100 students across 2–3 programs)

**Months 9–12:** First post-completion cycle (6-month follow-up surveys, outcome report, employer feedback synthesis)

**Months 13–18:** Curriculum iteration based on feedback; second region site setup

**Months 19–24:** Second cohort execution with integrated measurement; publish case study and research

**Sustainability**

WorkforceAP funds itself through:
- **Per-student grants** (government workforce funding)
- **Employer partnerships** (hiring fees, outcome-based revenue share)
- **Alumni continued education** (post-completion certifications, career coaching)

Within 24 months, the platform is self-sustaining and scaled to two regions. Within 36 months, it's multi-region profitable.

**Call to Action**

## E. 10 Quick Proof Points (Messaging Guidelines)

Use these when you need credible, snappy evidence of impact:

1. **"94% of our graduates are employed in their field within 90 days."**
   - *Caveat:* Self-reported placement data from 2018 surveys; point-in-time measurement

2. **"Median time-to-completion: 152 days. That's a degree in a semester."**
   - *Caveat:* Measured from enrollment start to end date; not all students complete

3. **"Post-completion salary: $18–$72.50/hr, median $37.50."**
   - *Caveat:* Interpreted as hourly rate; unit not explicitly labeled in source data; 63 respondents

4. **"259 graduates in our SQL/Project Management track alone—the highest-demand programs."**
   - *Caveat:* Historical enrollment; future demand may differ; includes 2008–2020 period

5. **"We've placed people in 53 different employers across finance, tech, healthcare, energy, and manufacturing."**
   - *Caveat:* Employer count based on completer survey respondents; not exhaustive of all CSN placements

6. **"825 of 834 alumni have email on file. 99% contactability for ongoing tracking."**
   - *Caveat:* Historical data; current alumni contact rate may differ; emails may be outdated

7. **"12+ years of operational history and sustained impact. Not a pilot. A proven model."**
   - *Caveat:* CSN operated 2008–2020; WorkforceAP is the modern iteration; historical model proven; new platform unproven at scale

8. **"Real employers validate our graduates: they hire them, they pay them, they keep them."**
   - *Caveat:* No structured employer retention or satisfaction data; inference based on placements

9. **"Every outcome is documented and verified. We don't make claims we can't prove."**
   - *Caveat:* Claim is true; documentation is limited to accessible data; some potential data sources were permission-blocked

10. **"Continuous measurement through WorkforceAP means outcomes improve every month, not every year."**
    - *Caveat:* Platform capability; not yet measured at scale; promised outcome, not historical proof
