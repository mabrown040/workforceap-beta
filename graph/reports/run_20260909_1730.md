## What remains open

- **Clean migration replay:** duplicate historical DDL and the subsequent ordering failure prevent a reproducible fresh replay. The fail-closed migration guard and recovery runbook mitigate operator error but do not fix migration history. Continue in an isolated database with explicit postconditions; do not rewrite applied historical checksums or declare failed migrations applied without proving their schema effects.
- **Restore proof:** the [read-only backup snapshot](../evidence/deps-production-backups-run_20260909_1730.txt) showed eight completed daily backups, latest September 9 at 09:54:37 UTC, and PITR disabled. Backup metadata is not evidence of a successful restore. A controlled restore drill and measured recovery outcome remain outstanding. See [database recovery runbook](../../docs/DATABASE-RECOVERY.md).
- **Operational delivery:** recipient inbox delivery and any uncertain historical send require provider evidence or staff reconciliation. No generic email outbox, universal exactly-once guarantee, or delivery webhook system is claimed.
- **Other audit findings:** older below-slice claims remain outside this authorized patch batch. Their original status and ranking are preserved.

## Proposed durable rules

Ten new rules are appended to [rules.json](../rules.json), all with `status: proposed`, each linked to its verified supersession:

1. Reject deleted identities throughout shared authentication and request context.
2. Clear local logout state independently of global provider revocation.
3. Enforce staff MFA setup/verification and deny access when assurance cannot be read.
4. Persist milestone attempts and receipts, preserve provider keys, constrain retries and finalize complete ledgers without sending.
5. Surface resolved partner provider errors and await best-effort failure diagnostics.
6. Align deployment-health HTTP status with its measured result.
7. Do not consume outreach success state after email rejection.
8. Require training access before assigning a non-start risk penalty.
9. Show unreadable diagnostics as unavailable.
10. Make required CI build every separately locked deployment package.
