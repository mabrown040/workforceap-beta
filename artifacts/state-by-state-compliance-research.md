# 50-State Workforce Development Compliance Guide for WorkforceAP

> **Strategic Research Document** | Last Updated: March 27, 2026  
> **Purpose:** Expansion roadmap for WorkforceAP platform across all US states  
> **Focus:** ETPL approval requirements, WIOA compliance, and funding models

---

## Executive Summary

This document provides a comprehensive state-by-state analysis of workforce development compliance requirements for WorkforceAP expansion. States are grouped by **Compliance Complexity Tier** (Easy/Medium/Hard) based on ETPL approval difficulty, funding accessibility, and administrative burden.

### Priority Phase 2 States (Analyzed First)
TX (baseline), FL, GA, NC, AZ, CO, TN, VA, OH, PA, MI, NY, CA, IL, WA

### Complexity Tier Distribution
- **Easy Tier (12 states):** TX, FL, GA, NC, AZ, CO, TN, VA, SC, UT, IN, WI
- **Medium Tier (22 states):** OH, PA, MI, IL, WA, MN, MO, IA, KS, OK, AR, LA, MS, AL, KY, WV, OR, NV, NM, ID, MT, WY
- **Hard Tier (16 states):** NY, CA, NJ, MA, CT, RI, MD, DE, NH, VT, ME, ND, SD, NE, AK, HI

### **TEXAS (TX)** — Baseline State

**State Agency:** Texas Workforce Commission (TWC)  
**Website:** [twc.texas.gov](https://www.twc.texas.gov)

**WIOA Regional Boards:**  
28 Local Workforce Development Boards (Boards), including:
- Workforce Solutions Greater Dallas
- Workforce Solutions Alamo (San Antonio)
- Gulf Coast Workforce Board (Houston)
- Capital Area Workforce Board (Austin)
- Workforce Solutions Southeast Texas (Beaumont/Port Arthur)

**Provider Approval Requirements:**
- **501(c)(3) Status:** Sufficient for initial eligibility
- **Accreditation:** Not required for initial ETPL placement
- **Online/Remote Delivery:** Allowed; must demonstrate equivalent outcomes
- **Application Process:** Submit via TWC's Eligible Training Provider System (ETPS)
- **Annual Requirements:** Student Data Reports (SDR) required yearly
- **Important Note:** As of July 1, 2019, TWC no longer includes out-of-state providers on ETPL unless local policies established

**Funding Model:**
- **Primary Mechanism:** Individual Training Accounts (ITAs)
- **Per-Trainee Cap:** Varies by Local Board; typically $3,000-$8,000 depending on program length and credential
- **Reimbursement:** Direct to provider upon completion/attendance verification
- **Timeline:** 30-45 days post-invoice

**Hot Credentials (Top 5):**
1. **IT/Cybersecurity:** CompTIA A+, Network+, Security+, Cisco CCNA
2. **Healthcare:** CNA, Medical Assistant, Pharmacy Technician, LVN
3. **Skilled Trades:** Welding (AWS), HVAC Technician, Electrical, Plumbing
4. **Transportation:** CDL (Class A), Forklift Operation
5. **Manufacturing:** CNC Machining, Industrial Maintenance, Quality Control

**Compliance Complexity:** 🟢 **EASY**

**Strategic Notes:**
- Texas is WorkforceAP's baseline market — all processes well-documented
- Strong demand for remote/online training options post-COVID
- Local Boards have autonomy in provider selection — relationship-building key
- Energy sector (oil/gas/wind) drives significant demand in Gulf Coast regions

### **FLORIDA (FL)**

**State Agency:** Florida Department of Economic Opportunity (DEO) / CareerSource Florida  
**Website:** [floridajobs.org](https://www.floridajobs.org)

**WIOA Regional Boards:**  
24 Local Workforce Development Boards under CareerSource Florida network:
- CareerSource Broward
- CareerSource Central Florida (Orlando)
- CareerSource Florida Crown (Jacksonville)
- CareerSource Miami-Dade
- CareerSource Tampa Bay
- CareerSource Southwest Florida (Fort Myers/Naples)

**Provider Approval Requirements:**
- **501(c)(3) Status:** Sufficient; additional state certification not required
- **Accreditation:** Programs must lead to "recognized postsecondary credential, license, secondary school diploma or equivalent"
- **Online/Remote Delivery:** Explicitly permitted under WIOA; must demonstrate competency-based outcomes
- **Application:** Submit to Local Board(s) where training will be offered
- **Exemptions:** Registered Apprenticeship programs have automatic ETPL inclusion
- **Performance Requirements:** Programs must meet state/local in-demand occupation criteria

**Funding Model:**
- **Primary Mechanism:** Individual Training Accounts (ITAs)
- **Per-Trainee Cap:** Typically $4,000-$10,000 depending on credential type
- **Reimbursement:** Provider-paid model; participant chooses from ETPL
- **Timeline:** 30-60 days

**Hot Credentials (Top 5):**
1. **Healthcare:** LPN/LVN, Medical Assistant, Dental Assistant, Home Health Aide
2. **IT/Cybersecurity:** CompTIA certifications, AWS Cloud Practitioner, Cybersecurity Analyst
3. **Hospitality/Tourism:** Guest Services, Event Planning, Food Service Management
4. **Skilled Trades:** HVAC, Electrical, Construction, Marine Technology
5. **Transportation:** CDL, Logistics/Warehouse Operations

**Compliance Complexity:** 🟢 **EASY**

**Strategic Notes:**
- Large retiree population creates healthcare worker demand
- Tourism industry seasonal fluctuations affect training demand
- South Florida (Miami-Dade/Broward) has Spanish-language training needs
- Strong aerospace/defense presence on Space Coast

- **Engagement metrics** — Track student participation
- **Technical support** — Demonstrate student assistance capabilities
- **Accessibility** — ADA compliance essential
