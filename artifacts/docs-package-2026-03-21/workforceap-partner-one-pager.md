# WorkforceAP Partner One-Pager

## What WorkforceAP offers partners
WorkforceAP is building a stronger way for community organizations, churches, nonprofits, workforce groups, and other referral partners to support people from interest to training to employment.

The platform is designed to give partners more than a referral handoff.
It is meant to provide visibility, accountability, and a clearer path to outcomes.

## Why this matters
In many workforce ecosystems, partners can refer people into programs but have very little visibility after that.

That creates common problems:
- referrals disappear into a black box
- progress is hard to track
- outcomes are hard to prove
- partner contribution is hard to measure
- strong partnerships and weak partnerships often look the same on paper

WorkforceAP is being built to solve that.

## What partners can do
Depending on rollout and access, WorkforceAP is designed to support partners by helping them:
- refer participants
- track assigned participants and cohorts
- monitor progress
- understand completions and placements
- get clearer accountability across the workforce journey

## Why this is valuable
A strong partner workflow is not just about visibility.
It is about being able to answer practical questions like:
- Did the people we referred actually engage?
- Are they progressing?
- Are they completing?
- Are they getting placed?
- Where do they need more support?

That turns a partnership from a loose relationship into an accountable operating model.

## What makes WorkforceAP different
WorkforceAP is not just trying to collect leads.
It is building toward a system that helps partners stay connected to real participant progress and outcomes.

That creates better conditions for:
- stronger partner trust
- better intervention when participants stall
- clearer renewal/value conversations
- more measurable workforce collaboration

## Current partner product direction
The current partner-focused priorities include:
- consistent partner portal positioning and naming
- visibility into assigned members and progress
- subgroup/cohort support for organizations and managers
- stronger reporting around completions, placements, and partner performance
- eventually, more advanced accountability and white-label potential

## Who this is best for
WorkforceAP partner workflows are especially relevant for:
- workforce boards
- community nonprofits
- churches and community organizations
- referral-based workforce support groups
- ecosystem partners that need visibility into outcomes

## Long-term direction
Over time, the partner layer can expand into:
- stronger reporting and accountability
- subgroup and cohort management
- renewal-oriented proof of value
- partner-branded experiences
- scalable B2B platform value

## Bottom line
WorkforceAP is building a partner experience that helps organizations do more than refer people — it helps them stay connected to progress, accountability, and outcomes.
