Mission-Driven Advocacy

With over a quarter-century of expertise in workforce development, Michael Brown has become a synonymous name with progress in Austin’s socio-economic landscape. As a Project Management Professional (PMP), he applies rigorous structural methodologies to solve human problems.

Michael founded the Workforce Advancement Project to bridge the widening gap between high-growth industries and underrepresented communities. His mission-driven approach focuses on educational equity—ensuring that every Austinite, regardless of zip code, has a seat at the table of the new economy.
