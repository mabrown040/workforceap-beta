Our mission

Workforce Advancement Project (WorkforceAP) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that equips individuals, nonprofits, counselors, and community advisors with workforce readiness tools, occupational training and professional certificates, and AI-powered resume, interview, and job placement assistance to help secure meaningful employment, build lasting careers, and a better life no cost to the member.

Start with Find Your Path, see programs that fit, and get advisor guidance, resume help, and job-search support.
