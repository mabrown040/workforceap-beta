# Dad Demo Script — 5-Minute Partner Discovery Walkthrough
**Version:** 2026-05-06 · For Michael Brown Sr.  
**Purpose:** Show a prospective partner what WorkforceAP is, how the portal works, and why a referral partnership makes sense.  
**Contingency:** If the live portal misbehaves, fall back to backup slides (noted as [BACKUP]).

---

## Pre-Demo Checklist (2 minutes before call)

- [ ] Open [workforceap.org](https://www.workforceap.org) in one tab
- [ ] Open partner portal (`/partner`) in another tab (logged in as demo partner account)
- [ ] Have this script printed or on second screen
- [ ] Have partner one-pager PDF ready to screen-share if needed
- [ ] Test audio/video. Close Slack/email notifications.

---

## The Script (5 minutes, spoken)

---

### **MINUTE 0:00 — 0:30 · Hook**

> "Thanks for taking the time. I'm Michael Brown with Workforce Advancement Project — WorkforceAP for short. We're a 501(c)(3) nonprofit here in Austin, and we've been doing workforce development for over 25 years.
>
> "Today I'll show you exactly what a member experiences when someone you refer comes through our program — and what you see as a partner. It's about five minutes. Sound good?"

**[Pause for confirmation. Adjust if they want a different focus.]**

---

### **MINUTE 0:30 — 1:15 · The Problem We Solve**

> "Here's the situation we see every day: There are people in our community — bright, motivated, ready to work — who are stuck in low-wage jobs because they can't afford training, can't navigate the system, or don't know what's actually out there.
>
> "WorkforceAP fixes that by making career training **no-cost** for qualifying members. Not discounted. Not a loan. Actually no cost — funded by grants and partnerships.
>
> "We train them, certify them, and connect them to employers. You send us someone who needs a shot. We do the heavy lifting. You track the outcome."

---

### **MINUTE 1:15 — 2:30 · The Member Journey (Live or Backup)**

**[Screen-share: workforceap.org homepage]**

> "This is what a member sees when they come to the site."

**[Scroll slowly through hero, programs, trust badges]**

> "They learn who we are, what programs we offer — IT support, cybersecurity, project management, AI — and they see that this is real: we're a 501(c)(3), we've been doing this for decades, and we partner with Texas Workforce Commission, ReWork America Alliance, Goodwill, and the Urban League.
>
> "When they're ready, they click 'Apply.'"

**[Screen-share: /apply page]**

> "The application takes about ten minutes. We ask basic info, program interest, and a quick eligibility check. A counselor reviews it within one to two business days and reaches out personally.
>
> "No hidden fees. No credit card. No catch."

**[If portal is slow / broken → BACKUP: screen-share partner one-pager instead, jump to partner view]**

---

### **MINUTE 2:30 — 3:45 · The Partner Portal (Live or Backup)**

**[Screen-share: /partner dashboard]**

> "Now here's the part that matters to you as a partner. When you refer someone to WorkforceAP, you get your own partner portal."

**[Point to referred members count, attention queue, milestone summary]**

> "You can see every person you've referred. You can see where they are in the process — did they apply? Did they start training? Did they earn a certificate? Did they get placed in a job?
>
> "This is real-time. No waiting for a quarterly report. You log in and you know.
>
> "If someone needs attention — maybe they stalled in training, or they need an extra push — the portal flags it so you or our team can follow up."

**[If partner portal is buggy → BACKUP: show static screenshot from `docs/portal-screenshots/partner-desktop-1280.png`]**

---

### **MINUTE 3:45 — 4:30 · Partnership Tiers & Commitment**

> "We keep partnership simple. Three tiers:
>
> **Referral Partner** — you send us eligible members. We give you a co-branded landing page and a dashboard. That's it.
>
> **Service Partner** — if you also provide wraparound support — childcare, transportation, housing — we integrate our case notes so nobody drops through the cracks.
>
> **Strategic Partner** — deeper commitment, shared funding applications, quarterly reviews, priority pipeline. We sign an MOU and we grow together.
>
> "Most partners start at the referral level and scale up once they see the outcomes."

---

### **MINUTE 4:30 — 5:00 · Close & Next Step**

> "So here's what I propose: Let's schedule a 15-minute follow-up where I walk you through the partner portal with real data — not a demo, actual numbers — and we see if there's a fit.
>
> "If it makes sense, we draft a simple referral agreement. No legal complexity. Just a handshake in writing so your team knows the process.
>
> "What does your timeline look like? Are you looking to refer members now, or are you exploring for later this year?"

**[Pause. Let them talk. Take notes. Don't oversell.]**

## Objection Cheat Sheet (On-Call Response)

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already refer to other programs." | "That's good — means you care about outcomes. WorkforceAP is different because we track every referral in real time. You see what happens. Most partners use us alongside their existing pipeline." |
| "What's the catch?" | "No catch. We're a nonprofit. Members pay $0. We fund this through grants and employer partnerships. You don't pay us either." |
| "How do we know members actually get jobs?" | "The partner portal shows placement data. Plus we're building an employer hiring consortium — companies that specifically want to hire our graduates." |
| "This sounds like a lot of work for our staff." | "It's actually less work. You make the referral. We handle enrollment, training, and placement. Your staff just checks the dashboard." |
| "Can we try one referral first?" | "Absolutely. That's how most partners start. One referral, see the process, then scale." |
