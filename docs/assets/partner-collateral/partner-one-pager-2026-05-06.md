# Workforce Advancement Project
## Partner One-Pager · 2026-05-06

### What We Are

Workforce Advancement Project (WorkforceAP) is a **501(c)(3) nonprofit** (EIN 41-2612389) that equips individuals, counselors, and community ambassadors with workforce readiness tools, occupational training, professional certificates, and AI-powered resume, interview, and job placement assistance — **at no cost to qualifying members**.

Founded in Austin on 25+ years of workforce development experience.

---

### Who We Serve Together

| Audience | How We Help |
|----------|-------------|
| **Members** | 19 career training programs (CompTIA, Google, IBM, AWS, Microsoft), exam-fee support, AI resume/interview tools, counselor guidance, job placement |
| **Community Partners** | Referral tracking, co-marketing, outcome dashboards, shared pipeline visibility |
| **Employers** | Pre-screened, certified, job-ready candidates at zero placement cost |

### What Members Experience

1. **Apply** (10 minutes) — share background, interests, eligibility
2. **Match** — counselor assigns best-fit program based on goals and readiness
3. **Train** — online coursework + AI-powered career tools + live support
4. **Certify** — sit for industry exam; exam fees covered for qualifying members
5. **Place** — job matching, interview prep, employer introductions

Typical timeline: **3–6 months** from application to job-ready.
