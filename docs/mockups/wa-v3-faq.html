General Questions

What is Workforce Advancement Project (WorkforceAP)?

Workforce Advancement Project (WorkforceAP) is a national nonprofit and 501(c)(3) that helps members explore career training, industry credentials, and next-step support. Programs are offered through WorkforceAP and partner-backed pathways.

Learn About Our Mission
Is it really no-cost to members?

Programs are available at no cost to members. Exact eligibility and funding path can vary by program and situation, and WorkforceAP will walk you through the right next step during intake.

Who qualifies?

Eligibility depends on the program, funding path, location, and your situation. If you're unsure, start with the application and WorkforceAP can help you understand the best next step.

How long do programs take?

Most programs take 3–5 months at roughly 10 hours per week. Digital Literacy is shorter at about 6 weeks and roughly 30 total hours. Everything is designed so you can participate while working, parenting, or managing other commitments.

Applying & Eligibility

What if I'm not technical?

That's exactly who we built this for. Our programs start from zero — no coding background, no IT history, no prior tech experience required. We've helped career-changers, people re-entering the workforce, and adults who've never worked in tech land roles in IT, cybersecurity, and data analytics. What matters most is your commitment to showing up and finishing.

Take the Career Quiz
What if I'm starting over?

Starting over is one of the most common reasons people come to us. Whether you left a job, got laid off, are leaving a difficult situation, or are just ready for a different path — we work with you from where you are now. Your advisor will help you pick the right program, set a realistic pace, and stay on track through the whole process.

Programs & Training

How long do programs take?

Most programs take 3–5 months at roughly 10 hours per week. Digital Literacy is shorter at about 6 weeks and roughly 30 total hours. All programs are designed to be completed while working part-time or managing family responsibilities.

View Program Details
Is this online?

Yes. All training is delivered virtually — you can participate from home, a library, or anywhere with a reliable internet connection. You do not need to commute, relocate, or take time off work to attend. Some programs include optional in-person events and local career fairs if you want them.

See Available Programs
Do I need a laptop?

You need access to a computer and reliable internet to participate. If you do not have a laptop, apply anyway. WorkforceAP can talk through available support options, including laptop help when a pathway or funding source allows it.

Learn About Support
What certifications will I earn?

You'll earn industry-recognized certificates from partners like Google, IBM, Microsoft, Amazon, and CompTIA. These are the same credentials employers hire against.

View Certification Paths
What if I fail a certification exam?

Many certification providers allow retakes. We work with you to prepare for exams and, when available, support retake options. Your advisor can help you understand the specific retake policy for your program.

See How It Works

Job Placement

Do you help members find jobs?

Yes. WorkforceAP provides support like resume help, interview prep, career guidance, and employer-facing support as members move toward job readiness.

Explore Career Outcomes
What kind of jobs will I qualify for?

Entry-level to mid-level roles in IT, cybersecurity, data analytics, project management, healthcare, and skilled trades. Starting salaries range from $38K to $145K depending on the program.

How soon after completing the program can I get hired?

Hiring timelines vary by market, role, location, and the path you choose. Many members begin preparing and applying during training so they are ready for the next step as they finish.

For Members

What support do I get during training?

You receive a dedicated advisor, access to our member portal with career readiness tools (resume help, interview practice), and job-search guidance as you move toward job readiness.

Learn About Our Mission
Is there a counselor or advisor assigned to me?

Yes. Each member is assigned an advisor who supports you from intake through training and job-search preparation. Your advisor helps with program pacing, career goals, and connecting you to resources.

Meet the Team
Can I work while enrolled?

Yes. Programs are designed to be flexible for working adults. Most programs are paced for about 10 hours per week, and Digital Literacy is lighter at roughly 5 hours per week.

What if I fall behind?

Your advisor will work with you to adjust your pace. We're invested in your completion — not just your enrollment. Life happens, and we'll help you get back on track.
