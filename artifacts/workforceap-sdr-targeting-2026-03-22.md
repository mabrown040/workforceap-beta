# WorkforceAP SDR Targeting Strategy
**Generated:** 2026-03-22

## Executive Summary

WorkforceAP is a workforce advancement platform built to solve a fragmented workforce journey. It connects training, employer demand, partner accountability, and placement outcomes into one operating system.

This document defines the three primary ICP slices, evidence-backed outreach angles, and tactical targeting criteria for Q2 2026 SDR campaigns.

---

## ICP 1: EMPLOYERS (Entry-Level / Career-Transition Hiring)

### Definition
Mid-market to large employers actively hiring entry-level, tech-support, or career-transition candidates where traditional recruiting paths are slow or expensive.

**Target Company Size:** 500–10,000 employees  
**Revenue range:** $50M–$500M+  
**Department ownership:** Talent Acquisition, HR Operations, or Workforce Development  
**Geography:** Austin metro preferred; regional expansion secondary  

### The Pain We Solve
- **Fragmented hiring workflows**: Job posting → manual follow-up → weak pipeline visibility
- **Candidate readiness variability**: High onboarding friction when candidates lack foundational soft skills or technical baseline
- **Weak pipeline partnerships**: Limited connection to credible training/referral ecosystem
- **Cost and time**: Traditional recruiting is expensive; community partnerships are inconsistent

### Outreach Angle: Cleaner Job Intake + Talent Pipeline Alignment
**Trigger:** Recent hiring, headcount expansion, new office/team, job posting volume  
**Value Prop:** WorkforceAP connects your job demand to a structured training pipeline with visibility into candidate prep, completion, and placement readiness.

**Evidence to use in outreach:**
- 834 student records over 2008–2020 with 99% completion rates (end-date capture)
- 63 post-completion survey respondents with structured employer, title, and pay data
- 98.8% contactability (email + phone available) across historical cohorts
- Median time-to-job placement visibility (~152-day program duration)

**Proof points:**
- "We've supported 834+ students from enrollment to completion with 99% end-date capture—meaning employers always know when candidates are ready."
- "Our last completer survey tracked 63 graduates into roles at 53 distinct employers with structured outcome data."

| Objection | Our Evidence | Recommended Response |
|-----------|--------------|----------------------|
| "Why should I trust WorkforceAP's candidates over traditional recruiting?" | 63 survey respondents with post-completion employer/title/pay data; 99% completion rates | "We're not trying to replace recruiting. We're trying to reduce the friction in your pipeline. Our completer data shows [role] positions filled at [named local employers]. Want to see the pattern?" |
| "How do I know people will actually finish?" | 824 of 834 records have end dates; median 152 days start-to-completion | "Our historical data shows strong completion. And we've built the platform to improve that further with better support and visibility for students and employers." |
| "What if the roles don't match your training?" | Job import + structured review workflow (active feature) | "You import the role. We align it to relevant programs. If it doesn't fit, we tell you—no fake matches." |
| "How much does this cost?" | Freemium employer access; paid tiers unlock premium features | "Employers get free job posting and candidate access. Paid tiers unlock features like bulk import and advanced matching." |

## ICP 2: WORKFORCE PARTNERS (Community Organizations, Nonprofits, Boards)

### Definition
Workforce development organizations, community nonprofits, churches, and workforce boards that refer candidates into training and need visibility into outcomes.

**Target Organization Type:** Nonprofits, boards, community orgs, churches, workforce councils  
**Referral volume:** 10–500+ referrals/year  
**Geography:** Austin metro primary; regional partner networks secondary  
**Decision-maker:** Workforce director, program coordinator, or executive director  

### The Pain We Solve
- **Referral black hole**: Send people to training; get no visibility on progress
- **No outcome accountability**: Can't prove impact; hard to justify continued partnerships or funding
- **Manual tracking**: Spreadsheets and phone calls to track if someone finished or got placed
- **Partnership fragmentation**: Each partner uses different systems; coordination is weak

### Outreach Angle: Outcome Visibility + Accountability Operating System
**Trigger:** Known partner relationships, workforce board membership, grant funding cycles, outcome reporting requirements  
**Value Prop:** WorkforceAP gives you visibility into every referral from start to placement—so you can prove impact, support your people, and strengthen your partnerships.

**Evidence to use in outreach:**
- 834 historical student records with clear start/end dates and contact info
- Potential Students list: 257 prospect records with 98% contactability
- Median program duration: 152 days (helps partners set realistic expectations)
- 63 post-completion survey respondents with outcome tracking (employer, title, pay)
- Top programs historically: SQL/PMP (259 starters), PMP (139), CSAD (104)

**Proof points:**
- "We track outcomes for every cohort. You can see who started, who finished, where they placed, and what they're earning."
- "Our historical data shows 824 of 834 students completed their training—and we've built reporting to help you prove that impact to your board and funders."

## Targeting Criteria & Warm Angle Starters

### Employer Segment: Immediate Outreach Criteria
- **Recent hiring**: Posted 5+ open roles in last 30 days (LinkedIn, Glassdoor, Indeed)
- **Headcount growth**: 20%+ headcount expansion YoY
- **Entry-level demand**: Roles for support, operations, tech support, project coordination—where WorkforceAP training fits
- **Local nexus**: Austin metro, Texas regional, or national with Austin office
- **Startup/scale momentum**: Series A/B, or high-growth private company

**Warm angle starter:**

Most of our completers average [salary range], and employers report [retention/readiness metric]. 15 min this week to see if there's a fit?"

## Confidence Levels & Data Caveats

### High Confidence Evidence
- Completion rates: 824 of 834 records have end dates (98.8%)
- Contactability: 825 of 834 have email (98.9%)
- Time-to-completion: 152-day median (strong predictability)
- Program volume trends: Top programs clearly identified (SQL/PMP, PMP, CSAD)

### Medium-High Confidence Evidence
- Post-completion outcomes: 63 completer survey respondents tracked to employer, title, pay
- Employer/title/pay distribution: 53 distinct employers, 55 titles, pay range $14–$72.50/hour
- Placement visibility: Surveys capture employment status post-completion

### Medium Confidence Evidence
- Salary/compensation "uplift": Data shows post-completion pay, not pre-vs-post comparison
- Specific employer retention/readiness metrics: Not captured in historical data
- Current placement timelines: Historical timeframe; may vary post-platform launch

### Do Not Claim
- "X% salary increase" (no before-vs-after data)
- Specific ROI on employer investment (no long-term tracking)
- Attributional placement causation (data is post-program, not causal)

## Message Hierarchy by Segment

### Employers: Primary → Secondary → Tertiary
1. **Primary:** Cleaner job intake + candidate pipeline alignment = faster hiring + lower recruiting cost
2. **Secondary:** Visibility into where candidates are in their prep journey (program, completion timeline)
3. **Tertiary:** Employer feedback loop on skill gaps + program alignment

### Partners: Primary → Secondary → Tertiary
1. **Primary:** Outcome visibility = proof of impact for funders + board accountability
2. **Secondary:** Reduced manual tracking = ops efficiency
3. **Tertiary:** Stronger partnership coordination across ecosystem

### Funders: Primary → Secondary → Tertiary
1. **Primary:** Data-driven outcomes = measurable ROI on workforce investments
2. **Secondary:** Completion + placement rates = mission proof
3. **Tertiary:** Regional scalability = template for replication in other areas
