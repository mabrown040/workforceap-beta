Workforce Advancement Project (WorkforceAP) is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit that equips individuals, nonprofits, counselors, and community advisors with workforce-readiness tools, occupational training and professional certificates, and AI-powered resume, interview, and job-placement assistance — at no cost to qualifying members.
