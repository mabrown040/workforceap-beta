## Executive Summary
The call produced a lot of requests, but they are not equal.

**3-star** = basic credibility and clarity fixes that make the site look less confusing.
**7-star** = meaningful product features that improve operations, placement outcomes, and stakeholder trust.
**10-star** = category-defining capabilities that create real differentiation, monetization leverage, and SaaS potential.

If we try to build everything at once, we dilute the product. The right move is:
1. Ship the 3-star trust/clarity layer fast.
2. Prioritize the 7-star employer + partner + member operating system.
3. Treat 10-star items as strategic bets after the operational core is stable.
