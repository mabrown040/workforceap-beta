# WorkforceAP SDR Email & Call Sequences
**Generated:** 2026-03-22

## Sequence Overview

Three email sequences + two LinkedIn scripts + one follow-up call script, tailored to the three primary segments (Employers, Partners, Funders).

Each sequence is designed to:
- Open with a specific trigger or observation
- Lead with evidence-backed value prop
- Escalate through objection handling
- Close with low-friction CTA

All sequences include response tracking and objection reply paths.

## EMPLOYER SEQUENCES

### Employer Sequence 1: Hiring Momentum Angle (Cold → Warm)

**Best for:** Employers with recent job posts, team expansion, or hiring surge

**Assumptions:**
- Target has posted 5+ open roles in the last 30 days
- Roles are entry-level, support, or career-transition titles
- Austin metro or regional with local hiring

## FOLLOW-UP CALL SCRIPT

**Scenario:** Prospect accepted meeting (email → call 1:1)

**Duration:** 15 minutes

**Goal:** Qualify interest, identify pain, schedule next step (demo or deeper conversation)

---

### Call Flow: 15-Minute Discovery

#### Opening (1 min)
"Hi [First name], thanks for making time. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this brief. Our goal is just to understand if WorkforceAP might be useful for [specific pain: faster hiring / outcome visibility]. Sound good?"

---

#### Discovery (8 min)

**For Employers:**
1. "Tell me about your current hiring process for [specific role]. What's working? What's the biggest headache?"
2. "When you hire entry-level folks, what's the ramp time before they're productive?"
3. "Have you ever worked with training/workforce programs, or is it all direct recruiting?"

**Listen for:**
- High onboarding friction
- Expensive recruiting costs
- Openness to non-traditional sources
- Growth hiring urgency

**Probe deeper on hot spots:**
- "How many roles like that are you hiring for this year?"
- "What would it be worth to you to cut onboarding time in half?"
- "Would access to pre-trained candidates change your hiring strategy?"

---

**For Partners:**
1. "How do you currently track referrals after they enroll in training?"
2. "What do your funders ask you about outcomes?"
3. "How much time does your team spend manually tracking people?"

**Listen for:**
- Manual tracking burden
- Funder outcome demands
- Inability to prove impact
- Grant renewal urgency

**Probe deeper on hot spots:**
- "How many referrals a year are we talking about?"
- "What would it mean for your next funder conversation to have this data automated?"
- "Are you up for grant renewal this year or next?"

---

**For Funders:**
1. "What's the biggest challenge you see in tracking outcomes across your training programs?"
2. "How do you currently measure whether placements are real and sustained?"
3. "If you could get one data improvement, what would matter most?"

**Listen for:**
- Outcome transparency gaps
- Provider reporting inconsistency
- Sustain employment uncertainty
- RFP/grant cycle timing

**Probe deeper:**
- "When's your next RFP window?"
- "Would better outcome data change how you allocate funding?"
- "What does a 'good' placement look like to you?"

---

#### Pitch (4 min)

**For Employers:**
"Here's what we do: employers import jobs, we align them to training programs, you get visibility into candidate prep and readiness. Our completers historically place at [salary range], median time-to-productivity is [X days]. And you see real outcome data—not just promises."

**For Partners:**
"We give you a portal where referrals automatically feed in, you see progress in real time, and you can generate outcome reports for your board/funders. 98.8% of participants historically complete, and we track placement and outcomes."

**For Funders:**
"We operationalize outcome tracking. You get completion rates, placement verification, employer data, wage outcomes—all standardized so you can compare providers and measure real impact across your programs."

---

#### Close (2 min)

**If warm:**
"This seems like something worth exploring. What would the next step look like for you? [Demo? Deeper call with leadership? Trial access?]"

**If qualified but not ready:**
"Sounds like this might be useful down the line. Where are you in [grant cycle / hiring plan / evaluation window], and when would be a good time to revisit?"

**If not a fit:**
"Thanks for the time. If things change or you want to explore this later, you know where to find me."

## Response Patterns & Playbook Adjustments

### If reply rate is <8% (cold employer sequence):
- Revise subject line to be more specific (add company name or job title)
- Move trigger observation earlier in body
- Test different industry verticals
- Check list quality (ensure recent hiring signals)

### If reply rate is 8–15% (healthy):
- Keep sequences as-is
- Scale volume
- Test different CTA options ("15 min," "20 min," "no pressure")

### If acceptance rate is <10% (warm meetings):
- Objections in replies suggest a messaging issue
- Most common: "We already have a recruiting solution"
  - Reframe: "This isn't recruiting—it's about reducing onboarding friction and improving retention"
- Most common: "Can't commit budget right now"
  - Reframe: "No cost to explore. Let's see if there's a fit first"

### If partner sequence reply rate is <8%:
- Outcome visibility angle might be weak; test grant/renewal angle instead
- Consider warm referral sourcing (ask current partners for intros)

### If funder sequence reply rate is <3%:
- Timing is critical. Only outreach within 6–8 weeks of known RFP cycle
- Cold funders take 3–6 touches. Plan for longer nurture (30–60 days)
- Test direct mail (funder programs respond better to printed RFP/outcome overviews)
