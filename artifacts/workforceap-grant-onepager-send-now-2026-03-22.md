# WorkforceAP — One-Page Grant Narrative (Send-Now Draft)

## Organization Snapshot
WorkforceAP builds on a long-running workforce training mission in Central Texas. Historical records from 2008–2020 show sustained program delivery, high learner contactability, and measurable post-completion outcomes. WorkforceAP modernizes that legacy into a platform that helps job seekers, employers, and partners move faster from training to employment.

## The Problem
Too many learners complete training but face fragmented pathways into jobs. At the same time, employers struggle to connect open roles with ready candidates. Workforce systems often lack unified, trusted visibility across training, outcomes, and hiring workflows.

## The WorkforceAP Solution
WorkforceAP provides a practical bridge between training and employment:
- Organizes program pathways so learners can choose and progress with clarity.
- Helps employers publish and manage opportunities with less friction.
- Gives partners a shared workflow to support placement outcomes.
- Translates historical impact into an operational, measurable system.

## Evidence of Historical Impact (from currently accessible records)
From analyzed historical files:
- **834** student records in the latest master audit workbook.
- **152 days** median time from start to end date (where measurable).
- **63** respondents with numeric post-completion pay entries in completer surveys.
- **$37.50/hr** median post-completion pay among those respondents.
- **53** distinct workplaces represented in completer survey outcomes.
- **825** records with email and **830** with phone in core master sheet (high contactability).

## Requested Use of Funds
Funding will support WorkforceAP execution in three tracks:
1. **Data modernization and integrity**
   - Canonical identity mapping, historical record harmonization, KPI instrumentation.
2. **Placement acceleration workflows**
   - Faster employer job ingestion/review/publish and improved candidate matching surfaces.
3. **Outcome measurement and reporting**
   - Cohort completion, time-to-placement, compensation trend tracking, and partner-ready reporting.

## 12–24 Month Outcomes (Target Direction)
- Reduced time from learner completion to placement decision.
- Increased number of active employer opportunities available in-platform.
- Higher percentage of cohorts with complete, auditable outcomes data.
- Stronger partner confidence through transparent KPI reporting.

## Why This Matters Now
WorkforceAP is not starting from zero. The historical record demonstrates durable mission execution. This grant would convert that legacy into a modern, measurable system that scales impact for learners and employers.
