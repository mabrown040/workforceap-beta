## The One-Line Version

WorkforceAP is the operating system for workforce development — connecting underserved adults, the organizations that serve them, and the employers that need them, in one verified closed loop.

---

## The Problem Nobody Has Solved

Every year, billions of dollars flow through workforce development in the United States:
- **$3B+** in annual WIOA federal funding
- **$500M+** in corporate workforce investment programs
- Thousands of nonprofits, churches, workforce centers, and community orgs serving job-seekers

And yet the system is broken in the same three places it's always been broken:

**1. Fragmentation.** A person in Austin gets referred by their church, trained by a nonprofit, certified through a vendor, and placed by a staffing firm — and none of those organizations can see each other's data or verify outcomes. Every handoff is a fax, a spreadsheet, or a phone call.

**2. The wrong people are excluded.** Every major platform has a gate. Guild Education requires employer sponsorship. Handshake requires a college degree. NPower caps at age 29. The adult at 34 who got laid off, the veteran at 42 returning to the workforce, the single mother at 38 restarting her career — they fall through every gap.

**3. Funders can't see outcomes.** Government agencies, corporate sponsors, and grant-makers pour money into the system and get PDF reports 6 months later. They can't see in real time whether their investment is working. So funding goes to whoever writes the best grant, not whoever gets people hired.

## What We Built

WorkforceAP is a full-stack workforce platform with four integrated portals:

### Member Portal
The career operating system for job-seekers. AI-powered career pathing, skills assessments, certification tracking, employer job access, direct counselor messaging, and a longitudinal record that follows them for 180+ days after placement. Looks and feels like a consumer app. No age gate. No degree requirement. No cost to the member.

### Partner Portal
Built for the referral ecosystem — nonprofits, churches, workforce centers, federal one-stop centers, community organizations. Partners refer candidates, track their progress, and export outcome data to their own funders. Full referral attribution. Subgroup management for multi-division orgs.

### Employer Portal
Built for companies that want verified, job-ready talent. Post positions, search profiles by certification and skill, see match scores, track applications. Logo-branded job cards. Subgroup management for multi-division companies (IBM's IT division and IBM's data division see different pipelines). First-look hiring access for co-funding partners.

### Admin Portal
Full member lifecycle management — counselor assignment, program enrollment, placement recording, grant-ready reporting, AI matching diagnostics, and a real-time dashboard that distinguishes tool users from enrolled members from placed graduates. Built for WIOA compliance from day one.

## The Closed Loop Nobody Else Has

```
Partner refers member
    ↓
Member completes training → earns certification
    ↓
Employer searches verified profiles → hires member
    ↓
Placement recorded → partner sees outcome → funder gets proof
    ↓
Employer co-funds next cohort
```

Every other platform handles one or two of these steps. We handle all five — and the data stays connected across all of them. That's the network effect. That's why this scales.

## The Platform Play

WorkforceAP Austin is the proof case. Once outcomes are documented:

**Phase 1 (2026): Prove it in Austin**
- 50-100 placements documented
- WIOA funding secured
- 3-5 corporate partners co-funding cohorts
- Platform fully operational

**Phase 2 (2026-2027): License to aligned orgs**
- Austin Area Urban League → `aaul.workforceap.org`
- NPower Texas chapter → their own instance
- Goodwill chapters → multi-city rollout
- Each org gets: their own branded site, their own member/partner/employer data, their own admin team
- WorkforceAP charges: flat fee for nonprofits, per-seat for training orgs

**Phase 3 (2027+): Network effects kick in**
- Employers on the platform can hire from ANY org's verified graduates
- Partners in one city can refer to programs in another city
- Funders get a single dashboard across all licensee orgs
- The platform becomes the infrastructure layer for the entire workforce development industry

## Funding Model (Three Revenue Streams)

**1. WIOA / Government Grants**
Texas Workforce Commission and federal WIOA funding per enrolled/placed member. Requires state exemption + EdVera approval (timeline: end of May 2026).

**2. Corporate Co-Funding**
Companies fund cohorts in exchange for first-look hiring access. Pricing: $X per cohort seat, revenue-shared between WorkforceAP and the training delivery partner.

**3. Platform Licensing**
SaaS fees from orgs that license the platform. Nonprofit flat fee. Training org per-seat. Estimated: $500-2,000/month per org depending on member volume.
