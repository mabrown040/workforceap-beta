Question 2 of 5

Interview Question

Tell me about a time you had to troubleshoot a complex technical problem. Walk me through your process.

AI Feedback

✅ Strengths

  • Clear structure using the STAR method
  • Specific technical details mentioned
  • Demonstrated ownership of the problem

🎯 Improve

  • Quantify the impact (how long did it take? what was saved?)
  • Mention what you learned from the experience

💡 Example Strong Answer

"When our prod server went down at 2 AM, I isolated the issue to a misconfigured firewall rule within 20 minutes by checking logs systematically. I restored service in 45 minutes total, preventing ~$15K in lost transactions, and documented the fix for the team..."
