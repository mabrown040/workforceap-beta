Eligibility

Who can apply

WorkforceAP is designed for adults who face barriers to employment — including low-income individuals, people with employment gaps, those with limited or no formal work history, justice-involved individuals, dislocated workers, veterans, and adult learners seeking a career change. WIOA eligibility can unlock additional funding.
