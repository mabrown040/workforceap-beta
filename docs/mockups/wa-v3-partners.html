Partner FAQ

Partner FAQ

Who can become a partner?

Employers, workforce development boards, non-profit services, churches, community organizations, social service agencies, and educational institutions can partner with WorkforceAP to refer candidates or hire graduates.

Is there a cost to refer candidates?

No. There is no cost to refer someone. We welcome partners who want to connect individuals in their network with our career training programs, offered at no cost for qualifying members.

How do I refer someone?

Use the referral partner registration form on this page to get a partner portal and referral link. You can also email info@workforceap.org or call (512) 777-1808 with the candidate's name and contact information.

Can I hire WorkforceAP graduates?

Yes. We actively connect employers with job-ready graduates. Reach out to discuss your hiring needs and we can share candidate profiles and schedule introductions.

What reporting do partners receive?

Partners receive regular updates on referral status, program completion, and placement outcomes. Our digital platform provides real-time dashboards for tracking progress.

Do you share member contact info?

Only with explicit member consent, and only the fields you need for follow-up. Members can revoke at any time, and you will see consent state on every record.

Can we bulk-import referrals?

Yes — CSV upload with column mapping. We validate rows, deduplicate against existing members, and surface errors row-by-row before commit.
