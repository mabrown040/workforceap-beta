The point of all this

Not a coat of paint — a system.

Every screen here is built from the same handful of components reading the same tokens. That's what makes the whole site changeable in one place, consistent across 124 pages, and simple enough that a member — or your dad — can find and use what they need without thinking. It rolls out one page at a time, each one safe and reversible.
