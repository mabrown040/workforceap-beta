## About Section (Short, 90–120 words)
WorkforceAP is rooted in years of workforce training and learner support across Central Texas. Historical records show sustained delivery, broad learner engagement, and measurable post-completion outcomes. Today, WorkforceAP transforms that legacy into a platform that connects training pathways, employer opportunities, and partner execution in one place. Our goal is practical: help more people move from learning to earning, with transparent progress and accountable outcomes.
