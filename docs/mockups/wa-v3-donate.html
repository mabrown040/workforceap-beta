What your donation funds

Donations support the work around access, training, and placement

WorkforceAP is building a workforce engine for people who need a real shot at a better job. Donations help fund the member support and operational work that makes that possible.

Training access

Support the systems, guidance, and coordination that help qualified members start and stay in career training.

Career readiness

Help fund resume support, interview prep, accountability, and the human follow-through that keeps members moving.

Employer-connected outcomes

Support the employer outreach, operations, and placement work that turn training into hiring opportunities.

Simple giving suggestions

Member basics

Helps cover job-search materials, communications, and small support costs that keep a member moving.

Career readiness support
