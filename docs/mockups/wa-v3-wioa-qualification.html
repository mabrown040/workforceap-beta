What happens next?

  1. Submit your application and tell us what kind of work or training you want.
  2. A WorkforceAP advisor reviews your information and follows up within 1–2 business days in most cases.
  3. We talk through fit, eligibility, and the best next step for your situation.
  4. If needed, we guide you to screening, documentation, interview, or program-readiness steps.
  5. You get a clearer recommendation for training, support, and job-readiness next steps.
